DNS METHOD TO DETERMINE ENZYME ACTIVITY
1. Objective and Principle
* Principle: Amylase hydrolyzes starch into maltose/glucose (reducing sugars). The reducing sugars reduce DNS (yellow) to 3-amino-5-nitrosalicylic acid (reddish-brown) in an alkaline medium when heated. The maximum absorption is at 540 nm, and the OD is proportional to the amount of reducing sugars.
* Unit: 1 U = amount of enzyme that produces 1 µmol of reducing sugar per minute at 37 °C, at optimal pH.
* The DNS method is performed after the second extraction, precipitation, and gel filtration steps.
2. Basic Steps
2.1: Reagent Preparation
* DNS Solution: 1 g DNS + 30 g Rochelle salt + 20 mL 2 M NaOH, add water up to 100 mL, store in a dark bottle. 
* 1% Starch: 1 g soluble starch + 100 mL buffer, heat to dissolve, let cool.
* 1 mg/mL Standard: 100 mg maltose, adjust volume to 100 mL. 
* Cold Buffer: Phosphate, optimal pH of amylase (pH 6.8).
2.2: Extract 2 Dilution
Keep the Extract in ice. Formula: Dilution Factor (HSPL) = total volume / extract volume. Each level requires 2 mL (3 sample tubes + 1 enzyme blank, 0.5 mL per tube).

Total volume used is 2.7 mL out of 5 mL. The purpose is to find a dilution level where the OD falls within the standard curve range.
2.3: Standard Curve Construction
Prepare 6 tubes, 1 mL each according to C₁V₁ = C₂V₂, with C₁ = 1 mg/mL.

* Add DNS, boil for 5–10 minutes, cool in ice water, add 8 mL of water, and mix.
* Zero the machine using S0, measure OD540 nm.
* Plot OD against concentration, linear regression: y = 0.83x + 0.0017, R² = 0.9999 (should be ≥ 0.98).
2.4: Enzyme Reaction
---------------------------------------------
| |Tube | |Starch 1% | |Bufer | |Diluted enzyme | |DNS |
---------------------------------------------
| |Mẫu (×3) | |0,5 mL | |0 | |0,5 mL | |1 mL, thêm sau ủ |
---------------------------------------------
| |Blank enzyme (BE) | |0,5 mL | |0 | |0,5 mL | |1 mL, thêm trước enzyme |
---------------------------------------------
| |Blank cơ chất (BS) | |0,5 mL | |0,5 mL | |0 | |1 mL, thêm sau ủ |

Steps:
1. Add starch to the tube, pre-incubate at 37 °C for 5 minutes.
2. Add enzyme to the sample tube and BE (BE already contains DNS), add buffer to BS, start the timer.
3. Incubate exactly 10 minutes at 37 °C.
4. Add 1 mL of DNS to the sample tube and BS to stop the reaction.
5. Boil for 5–10 minutes, cool in ice water.
6. Add 8 mL of water, mix.
7. Measure OD540 nm (machine blank is S0).
Assumed Values:
* Sample OD (Avg of 3 tubes): 0.450
* OD BE: 0.015
* OD BS: 0.02
2.5: Calculations
2.5.1. "Undiluted" Level (Dilution Factor HSPL = 1)
* Result prediction: Since the undiluted enzyme concentration is very high, the amount of reducing sugar produced will exceed the highest concentration of the standard curve (S5 = 1.0 mg/mL). The sample OD value at this point will exceed the linear range of the spectrophotometer (usually > 2.0 or 3.0).
* Conclusion: According to the protocol rules, absolutely no extrapolation beyond the standard curve is allowed; if the OD exceeds S5, the enzyme must be further diluted and remeasured. Therefore, the result from the "Undiluted" tube will be discarded and not used to calculate the activity.
2.5.2. 1:5 Dilution Level (HSPL = 5)
* Assumed actual photometric value: Sample OD (Avg of 3 tubes) = 0.868; OD BE = 0.020; OD BS = 0.02.
Calculation steps:
* Corrected OD = Sample OD − OD BE − OD BS = 0.868 − 0.020 − 0.02 = 0.828.
* Amount of reducing sugar formed (calculated per 1 mL of reaction): (0.828 − 0.0017) / 0.83 = 0.996 mg. (The OD value of 0.828 is valid because it falls within the standard curve range from S0 to S5).
* Convert to µmol: (0.996 / 342) × 1000 = 2.912 µmol.
* Enzyme activity (U/mL): (2.912 µmol × 5) / (10 minutes × 0.5 mL) = 2.912 U/mL.
2.5.3. 1:10 Dilution Level
* Corrected OD = Sample OD − OD BE − OD BS = 0.450 − 0.015 − 0.02 = 0.415.
* Reducing sugar (mg/mL) = (Corrected OD − b) / a = (0.415 − 0.0017) / 0.83 = 0.498 mg/mL. This concentration is calculated per 1 mL of reaction (before DNS), on the same basis as the standard curve, so the sugar mass is 0.498 mg.
* Convert to µmol (maltose 342 g/mol): (0.498 / 342) × 1000 = 1.456 µmol.
* Activity (U/mL) = µmol × HSPL / (time × enzyme volume) = (1.456 × 10) / (10 × 0.5) = 2.912 U/mL.
* With Dilution Factor (HSPL) = 10, incubation time = 10 minutes, and loaded enzyme volume into the reaction = 0.5 mL.
2.5.4. 1:20 Dilution Level (HSPL = 20)
* Assumed actual photometric value: Sample OD (Avg of 3 tubes) = 0.238; OD BE = 0.010; OD BS = 0.02.
Calculation steps:
* Corrected OD = Sample OD − OD BE − OD BS = 0.238 − 0.010 − 0.02 = 0.208.
* Amount of reducing sugar formed (calculated per 1 mL of reaction): (0.208 − 0.0017) / 0.83 = 0.249 mg. (Valid because it falls within the standard curve range).
* Convert to µmol: (0.249 / 342) × 1000 = 0.728 µmol.
* Enzyme activity (U/mL): (0.728 µmol × 20) / (10 minutes × 0.5 mL) = 2.912 U/mL.
2.6. Explanation of Quantities
* OD540: absorbance at 540 nm, unitless.
* a, b: slope and intercept of the standard curve.
* HSPL (Dilution Factor): used to convert results back to the original Extract.
* Blank enzyme: subtracts endogenous sugar already present in the Extract.
* Blank substrate: subtracts the background caused by starch.
* U/mL: activity in 1 mL of Extract.
* U/mg: specific activity, equal to U/mL divided by mg protein/mL, used to compare purity between steps (requires protein measurement).
3. Summary of calculation results
1. Stage 1: Crude Extract (Extract 2)
Sample Parameters:
* Total volume: 20 mL
* Sample volume for assay: 0.2 mL
* Dilution factor (HSPL): 10
Photometric Data:
* Average OD Sample: 0.450
* OD Blank Enzyme (BE): 0.015
* OD Blank Substrate (BS): 0.020
Results:
* Corrected OD: 0.450 - 0.015 - 0.020 = 0.415
* Reducing sugar: (0.415 - 0.0017) / 0.83 = 0.498 mg/mL
* Reducing sugar (µmol): (0.498 / 342) * 1000 = 1.456 µmol
* Volumetric Enzyme Activity: (1.456 * 10) / (10 * 0.5) = 2.912 U/mL
* Total activity for precipitation: 2.912 U/mL * 49.8 mL = 145.02 U
2. Stage 2: Salt-precipitated Enzyme (Crude enzyme 2)
Sample Parameters:
* Total volume: 10 mL
* Sample volume for assay: 0.1 mL
* Dilution factor (HSPL): 20
Photometric Data:
* Average OD Sample: 0.751
* OD Blank Enzyme (BE): 0.020
* OD Blank Substrate (BS): 0.020
Results:
* Corrected OD: 0.751 - 0.020 - 0.020 = 0.711
* Reducing sugar: (0.711 - 0.0017) / 0.83 = 0.854 mg/mL
* Reducing sugar (µmol): (0.854 / 342) * 1000 = 2.497 µmol
* Volumetric Enzyme Activity: (2.497 * 20) / (10 * 0.5) = 9.988 U/mL
* Total activity: 9.988 U/mL * 10 mL = 99.88 U
* Recovery yield: (99.88 / 145.02) * 100 = 68.87%
3. Stage 3: Gel-filtered Enzyme (Total enzyme 2)
Sample Parameters:
* Load volume: 9.9 mL (Total load activity: 98.88 U)
* Volume after pooling: 20 mL

* Dilution factor (HSPL): 5

Results:

* Reducing sugar (µmol): (0.577 / 342) * 1000 = 1.687 µmol
* Volumetric Enzyme Activity: (1.687 * 5) / (10 * 0.5) = 1.687 U/mL
* Total activity: 1.687 U/mL * 20 mL = 33.74 U
* Recovery yield: (33.74 / 98.88) * 100 = 34.12%
