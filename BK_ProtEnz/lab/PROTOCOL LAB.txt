Procedure:

1.  **Weigh components separately:** Weigh each salt separately
    according to the calculated amounts for 1 L of buffer (137 mM NaCl,
    2.7 mM KCl, 10 mM Na2HPO4, and 1.8 mM KH2PO4).

2.  **Initial dissolution:** Dissolve all weighed salts in approximately
    800 mL of distilled water in a beaker using a magnetic stirrer.

3.  **pH adjustment:** Place the pH probe into the stirring solution.
    Carefully adjust the pH to 7.4 by adding 1 M HCl or 1 M NaOH
    dropwise.

4.  **Final volume bringing:** Top up the solution with distilled water
    to reach the final volume of 1000 mL (1 L), then mix thoroughly.

**MATERIAL PREPARATION**

- Wash the sweet potato, peel it, and cut it into small pieces.

- Place into a clean beaker and weigh exactly 20g.

- Measure 60mL of cold PBS buffer using a graduated cylinder.

**EXTRACTION & FILTRATION (1:3 W/V RATIO)**

> Procedure:

1.  **Pre-chilling setup:** Pre-chill the mortar, pestle, and PBS buffer
    on an ice bath (0-4°C) prior to extraction to prevent enzyme thermal
    denaturation.

2.  **Buffer volume allocation:** For 20 g of sample, a total volume of
    60 mL cold PBS buffer is required for extraction.

3.  **Sequential grinding:** Pour an initial small portion 30 mL of cold
    PBS buffer into the pre-chilled mortar to grind the sample into a
    smooth homogenate without splashing.

4.  **Filtration & residue rinsing:** Filter the homogenate through
    layers of cheesecloth. Use the remaining cold buffer in small
    portions to rinse the mortar, pestle, and the plant residue on the
    cheesecloth to maximize enzyme recovery.

5.  **Collection:** Collect the combined filtrate to reach the target
    volume of approximately 60 mL.

**Gel chromatography\**

#### **1. Resin Hydration & Degassing**

- Weigh the required dry resin matrix (e.g., Sephadex) and suspend in
  150 mL of PBS buffer. Allow the beads to swell completely (e.g., 3--6
  hours at room temperature, or 1 hour in a 90°C water bath depending on
  resin grade).

- Allow the resin to settle under gravity. Carefully decant the
  supernatant to remove fine, broken particles (\"fines\") that clog
  column frits.

- Resuspend settled beads in fresh PBS to create a 50%--75% slurry, and
  degas under a vacuum desiccator for 15--20 minutes to eliminate
  dissolved air.

#### **2. Column Packing & Equilibration**

- Mount the chromatography column vertically using a spirit level and
  clamp it securely in a vibration-free rack.

- Introduce 10 mL of degassed PBS into the bottom of the column to wet
  the net and eliminate trapped air bubbles below the frit.

- Pour the degassed slurry down the inside wall of the column in a
  single, continuous stream using a glass rod or packing reservoir.

- Open the column outlet to allow buffer to drain under gravity until
  the packed bed settles to a stable height corresponding to a final bed
  volume ($V_{c}$) of 80 mL.

- Equilibrate the column with 2 to 3 column volumes (160--240 mL) of
  degassed PBS to stabilize packing density. Verify that the effluent pH
  and UV baseline are stable.

#### **3. Sample Preparation & Loading**

- Clarify the crude sample by centrifugation at 4,000 × g for 15-20
  minutes at 4°C (or filter through a 0.45 µm low-protein-binding
  syringe filter).

- Drain the buffer head until the meniscus sits flush with the top of
  the settled bed surface, then close the outlet. *Do not allow the bed
  to dry.*

- Gently apply 1.0 to 1.6 mL of clarified supernatant (1.2%--2% of
  $V_{c}$) directly onto the center of the bed surface using a
  micropipette.

- Open the outlet to let the sample fully enter the resin bed. Wash the
  column walls with 0.5 mL of PBS and allow it to penetrate the bed
  before adding bulk buffer.

**4. Elution & Fractionation**

- Top up column with PBS buffer and maintain a constant gravity flow
  rate (\~1--2 mL/min).

- Allow the first 25 mL of effluent (void volume) to drain into a
  measuring cylinder/waste flask.

- Once 25 mL has drained, immediately begin collecting into 12 numbered
  tubes:

- Collect exactly 4.0 mL per tube (Tubes 1 to 12), spanning cumulative
  volume 25 mL to 73 mL.

- Store collected tubes on ice prior to spectrophotometric and enzymatic
  testing.

#### **5. Analytical Assays**

- **Total Protein Determination:** Measure absorbance at 280 nm
  (OD~280~) for all fractions using PBS as the optical blank to generate
  the protein elution profile.

- **Amylase Activity (DNS Method):**

  1.  *Enzymatic Reaction:* Combine 100 µL of fraction sample with 500
      µL of 1% (w/v) soluble starch solution in PBS. Incubate at 37°C
      for 10 minutes.

  2.  *Color Development:* Terminate the reaction by adding 1.0 mL of
      DNS reagent. Place the tubes into a boiling water bath (100°C) for
      5--10 minutes.

  3.  *Measurement:* Cool to room temperature, add 2.0 mL of deionized
      water to dilute if absorbance is off-scale, and read optical
      density at 540 nm (OD~540~) against a reagent blank (PBS
      substituted for sample).
