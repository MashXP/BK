\section{EXPERIMENTAL METHODS}
Proceed in the following order:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Open the valve for water to flow into the high-positioned tank until there is water in the overflowing tube.
    \item Open the valve for water to flow through the flow meter into the CSTRs system and adjust the flowrate.
    \item \textbf{System of one reactor:} When the system is stable, put the colored substance into reactor 1. Start the stopwatch at the same time. Use test tubes to take samples from time to time, then measure the absorbance.
    \item \textbf{System of two and three reactors:} Do the same as for one reactor; put the colored substance into reactor 1 and take samples at the last reactor (from the last interconnected pipe).
\end{enumerate}
