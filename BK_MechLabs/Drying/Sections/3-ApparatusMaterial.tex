\section{EXPERIMENTAL APPARATUS AND MATERIAL}
\subsection{Experimental apparatus}
The drying apparatus consists of:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Calorifer}: Has two dry resistors with a capacity of $10\text{KW}$. Temperature is stabilized by an automatic switch.
    \item \textbf{Sucking blower}: Sucking speed of $0.85\text{m/s}$ for loading moist air (drying agent) and blowing through the calorifer to raise the temperature.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Material}
Filter paper is used as the material for drying.
