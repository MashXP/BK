\section{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Crushing a material; based on the sieve results, determine the size distribution of the material after grinding, the power consumption, and the productivity of the mill.
    \item Sieve the material after crushing; determine the sieve efficiency, build the cumulative distribution curve of the material after grinding, and then determine the material size after grinding.
    \item Mix two materials to determine the mixing index at several time intervals; build the mixing index graph over time to determine the appropriate mixing time.
\end{itemize}
