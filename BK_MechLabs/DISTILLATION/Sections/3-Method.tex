\section{EXPERIMENT EQUIPMENT AND METHOD}

\subsection{Experiment Equipment}

The experiment uses a continuous distillation column with the following components:
\begin{itemize}
    \item 5-stage batch column.
    \item Reboiler with heating element.
    \item Condenser and reflux splitter.
    \item Feed pump and preheater.
    \item Temperature sensors at various stages.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Experiment Method}

The experiment was conducted according to the following steps:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Prepare the binary mixture of ethanol and water with a known concentration.
    \item Start the heating system and wait for the column to reach steady state.
    \item Adjust the feed flow rate and reflux ratio according to the experimental matrix.
    \item Measure temperatures and collect samples from the distillate and bottoms for concentration analysis.
    \item Repeat for different reflux ratios and feed locations.
\end{enumerate}
