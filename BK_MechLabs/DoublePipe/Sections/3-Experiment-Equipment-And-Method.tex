\section{EXPERIMENT EQUIPMENT AND METHOD}\label{experiment-equipment-and-method}

\subsection{Experiment equipment}\label{experiment-equipment}

There are 3 types of heat transfer surfaces:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Type A:} Elastic tube.
    \item \textbf{Type B:} Fluid flows vertically over the external surface of the inner tube. The two flows are perpendicular to each other.
    \item \textbf{Type C:} Simple tube, fluid flows longitudinally over the external surface of the inner tube. The two flows are parallel to each other.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Experimental Procedure}\label{method}

The experiment is conducted following these steps:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Get to know the double-pipe heat exchanger, thermometers, and flow meters.
    \item Supply water into the boiler and verify the water level is sufficient.
    \item Turn on the power supply and confirm the heater is functioning.
    \item Open the valves for both hot and cold water streams.
    \item Adjust the hot water flow rate ($G_1$) using the reflux valve and the cold water flow rate ($G_2$) as per the experimental matrix ($4, 6, 8, 10, 12$ L/min).
    \item Wait for the system to reach a steady state.
    \item Observe and record the inlet and outlet temperatures for both streams ($T_{1,in}, T_{1,out}, T_{2,in}, T_{2,out}$) once stability is reached.
    \item Note that the hot stream is refluxed continuously to maintain temperature. All experiments are conducted in counter-current flow mode.
\end{enumerate}
