\section{ABSTRACT}\label{abstract}

\subsection{Purposes}\label{purposes}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Get to know double-pipe heat exchanger, thermometers and flow meters.
    \item Determine heat transfer coefficient of heat exchanging between two hot and cold streams through a metal wall at different operation modes.
    \item Establish heat balance.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Methods}\label{methods}

\begin{quote}
    Study tube B and tube C at different operating modes, followed by calculating heat transfer coefficient between hot and cold fluid streams.
\end{quote}

\subsection{Results}\label{results}

\begin{quote}
    Raw result: $1 \text{ ft}^3 = 0.02832 \text{ m}^3$
\end{quote}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{\textbf{Tube B:}} Fluid flows vertically over the external surface of the inner tube. The two flows are perpendicular to each other.
    \item \emph{\textbf{Tube C:}} Simple tube, fluid flows longitudinally over external surface of the inner tube. The two flows are parallel to each other.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Result evaluation}\label{result-evaluation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item There are deviations between experimental data and theory due to errors from equipment and during the process of carry out the experiments.
    \item Heat transfer in tube type C is less efficient than in tube type B.
    \item Experimental heat transfer coefficient $K_l$ is lower than overall heat transfer coefficient $K_l^*$ because of heat loss and errors happening during experiment.
\end{itemize}
