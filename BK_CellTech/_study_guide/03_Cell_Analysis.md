## 1. Cell Counting Methods
Determining cell number and density is crucial for assessing growth.

### Direct Methods
*   **Haemocytometer (Neubauer chamber)**:
    *   Manual counting under a microscope.
    *   Uses **Trypan Blue** dye exclusion (dead cells take up dye, live cells exclude it).
    *   **Formula**: `Cells per ml = (Total count * 10^4) / Number of squares` (Standard formula implies dividing by squares counted, typically 4 or 5 depending on protocol).
*   **Automatic Image Analysis**:
    *   e.g., Sorcerer System.
    *   Automated counting of colonies or cells on plates.

### Indirect Methods
Used when direct counting is difficult (e.g., colonies in agar, bioreactors).
*   **Protein Determination**: Lowry & Bradford assays.
*   **DNA Determination**: Using fluorescent reagents like Hoechst 33258.
*   **Glucose Consumption**: Measurement of glucose oxidase + peroxidase activity to estimate metabolic activity.

## 2. Viability Measurement
Assessing the proportion of living cells.

### Techniques
1.  **Dye Exclusion**: Trypan blue (Live = Clear, Dead = Blue).
2.  **MTT Assay**: Mitochondrial activity reduces MTT (yellow) to formazan (purple).
3.  **LDH Determination**: Lactate Dehydrogenase leakage indicates loss of membrane integrity (cell death).
4.  **Intracellular Energy Charge**: Measuring ATP levels (Luciferase/Chromatography).
5.  **Protein Synthesis Rate**: Incorporation of radioactive isotopes (3H-leucine, 35S-methionine).
