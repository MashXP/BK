# Chapter 5: Plant Cell Culture Techniques

## 1. Callus

### 1.1. Callus
- **Definition**: A mass of unorganized, undifferentiated cells capable of division.
- **Causes**: Wounding + Plant growth regulators (auxin or auxin + cytokinin).

### 1.2. Purpose of Callus Formation
a. Micropropagation
b. Secondary Metabolite Production

### 1.3. Factors Affecting Callus Formation and Growth
- **Plant growth regulators**: Auxin/ auxin + cytokinin
- **Explant age**: Young organs/tissues
- **Explant type**: (Listed as a factor)
- **Light**: Dark condition

### 1.5. Stages of Callus Culture
1. Explant Selection and Collection
2. Explant Surface Sterilization
3. Inoculation of Explant
4. Subculturing

## 2. Cell Suspension Cultures

### 2.1. Cell Suspension
- **Definition**: A population of undifferentiated plant cells growing in a liquid medium.

### 2.2. Purpose of Establishing Cell Suspension Cultures
- Micropropagation
- Production of secondary metabolites

### Advantages & Disadvantages: Suspension Culture vs. Callus Culture

#### Advantages
- Faster growth
- Better nutrient & oxygen exchange
- Easier scale-up

#### Disadvantages
- Requires constant shaking
- Cells can be fragile
- Contamination

### 2.3. Methods for Establishing Cell Suspension Cultures
1. **Source**: Callus, seedlings, embryo, shoot meristem
2. **Process**: Shaken on orbital shaker (medium with auxin/ auxin + cytokinin)
3. **Result**: Cell suspensions

### 2.4. Factors affecting cell suspension culture establishment
- **Shaker speed**: Depending on flask shape and size
- **Inoculum density**:
    - Too low → reduces cell division (due to loss of "community effect")
    - Too high → nutrient competition
- **Growth regulators, light, temperature**: Similar requirements to callus induction stage

### 2.5. Growth Curve
- **Phases**: Lag phase → Exponential phase → Deceleration phase → Stationary phase
- **Optimal Subculturing Timing**: Transfer during the **early stationary phase**.

### 2.6. Biomass determination
Methods include:
- SCV (settled cell volume)
- PCV (packed cell volume)
- Fresh weight (FW)
- Dry weight (DW)
- Cell counting
- Electrical conductivity
- pH
