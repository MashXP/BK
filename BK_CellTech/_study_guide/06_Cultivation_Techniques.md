# Cultivation Techniques
**Date:** 2026-02-06
**Source:** AlCellTech.pdf (Slides 56-66)

## 1. Microcarrier Culture
Allows anchorage-dependent cells to be grown in suspension (bioreactors) by providing a surface on small floating beads.

### Characteristics
*   **Beads**: Dextran, cellulose, or plastic.
*   **Concentration**: e.g., Cytodex (2x10^5 beads/mL).
*   **Conditions**: Requires gentle stirring (50-70 rpm) to keep beads in suspension without shear damage.

### Types of Microcarriers
*   **Cytodex 1**: Dextran matrix, positively charged (attracts cells).
*   **Cytodex 3**: Coated with Collagen type I (mimics ECM).
*   **Cytopore**: Macroporous cellulose (cells grow inside pores, protected from shear).
*   **Cytoline**: High density, silica/polyethylene.

### Harvesting
Separating cells from beads can be challenging.
*   **Detachment**: Chelating agents (EDTA), Enzymes (Trypsin), Hypotonic shock.
*   **Separation**: Differential sedimentation (beads settle faster), Filtration (100µm mesh).

## 2. Cell Encapsulation
Enclosing cells within a semi-permeable membrane.
*   **Purpose**:
    *   **Immune Isolation**: Protects transplanted cells from the host immune system (antibodies/immune cells cannot enter).
    *   Allows diffusion of nutrients, oxygen, and therapeutic products (e.g., insulin).
*   **Types**:
    *   Solid capsules (Alginate).
    *   Membrane-coated solid/hollow spheres.
    *   Hollow silica/inorganic hybrids.

## 3. Hanging Drop Method
*   Used for generating 3D spheroids or Embryoid Bodies (EBs) from stem cells.
*   Gravity concentrates cells at the bottom of a droplet to promote aggregation.
