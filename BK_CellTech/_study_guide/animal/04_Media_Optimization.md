# Media Optimization (Serum-Free)
**Date:** 2026-02-06
**Source:** AlCellTech.pdf (Chapter 3/4, Slides 46-49)

## 1. Serum in Culture Media
Serum (e.g., FBS) is a common supplement but has significant drawbacks.

### Advantages
*   High growth-promoting activity (contains attachment factors, micronutrients, hormones, proteases).
*   High albumin content protects cells from shear forces and pH fluctuations.

### Disadvantages
*   **Batch-to-batch variation**: Inconsistent composition affecting reproducibility.
*   **High protein content**: Hinders downstream product purification (e.g., purifying recombinant antibodies).
*   **Contamination risk**: Potential source of viruses, prions (BSE), or mycoplasma.
*   **Cost**: Expensive and availability fluctuates.
*   **Ethical concerns**: Collection methods.

## 2. Serum-Free Media Strategies
Replacing serum requires identifying and adding specific "mitogenic components."

### Replacements
*   **Peptide Hydrolysates**: Plant/yeast derived.
*   **Insulin & Insulin-Like Growth Factor (IGF)**: Stimulate glucose uptake and growth.
*   **Epithelial Growth Factor (EGF)**: Activates mTORC1 pathway (Protein synthesis, cell growth).
*   **Transferrin**: Iron delivery.
