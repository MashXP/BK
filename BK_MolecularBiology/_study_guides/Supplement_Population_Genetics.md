# Supplement: Population Genetics

## 1. Inheritance in Self-fertilizing Populations
For a population starting with heterozygotes ($Aa$), the genotype frequencies at generation $n$ are:
$$(2^n - 1) AA : 2 Aa : (2^n - 1) aa$$
- As $n$ increases, the proportion of heterozygotes ($Aa$) decreases, while homozygotes ($AA$ and $aa$) increase.

---

## 2. Inheritance in Random Mating Populations (Hardy-Weinberg Principle)
In a large, random-mating population with no selection, mutation, or migration, allele and genotype frequencies remain constant.
- **Genotype Frequencies**: $p^2 AA : 2pq Aa : q^2 aa$
- Where:
    - $p$: frequency of the dominant allele ($A$).
    - $q$: frequency of the recessive allele ($a$).
    - $p + q = 1$.
    - $p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$.

---

## 3. Determining Population Equilibrium
- **Chi-square ($\chi^2$) Test**: Used to determine if the observed genotype frequencies significantly differ from the expected Hardy-Weinberg frequencies.
- **Equilibrium Coefficients**: Calculating various parameters to assess population stability.
