# Chapter 3: Transcription and Regulation

### 1. Gene Structure
*   **Components**: Promoter, RNA coding sequence, Terminator.
*   **Promoter Regions**:
    *   **Prokaryote**: -35 (TTGACA) and -10 (TATAAT/Pribnow box).
    *   **Eukaryote**: TATA box (at -30).

### 2. Transcription Mechanism
*   **Prokaryote**:
    *   **Initiation**: Sigma ($\sigma$) factor binds promoter (-35, -10), recruits RNA Polymerase holoenzyme.
    *   **Elongation**: Transcription bubble moves.
    *   **Termination**:
        *   **Rho-independent**: Stem-loop structure forms (C-G rich), followed by U-rich tail.
        *   **Rho-dependent**: Rho protein (helicase) binds C-rich region, catches up to polymerase, unwinds hybrid.
*   **Eukaryote**:
    *   **Initiation**:
        *   **Pre-initiation complex (PIC)**: Assembly of General Transcription Factors (GTFs) and RNA Pol II at the promoter.
        *   **Formation Sequence**: TFIID (contains TBP - TATA-binding protein) binds TATA box $\rightarrow$ TFIIA/TFIIB recruit RNA Pol II and TFIIF $\rightarrow$ TFIIE/TFIIH join to complete PIC.
        *   **TFIIH**: Acts as a helicase (unwinds DNA) and kinase (phosphorylates Pol II CTD).
        *   **Mediator Complex**: A co-activator that bridges distal enhancers/activators to the PIC.
    *   **Elongation**: FACT complex removes histones. C-terminal domain (CTD) phosphorylation.
    *   **Termination**: Poly-A signal (AAUAAA) triggers cleavage and polyadenylation.
    *   **RNA Polymerases**:
        *   **Pol I**: rRNA (except 5S).
        *   **Pol II**: **pre-mRNA** (primary transcript), miRNA, lncRNA.
        *   **Pol III**: 5S rRNA, tRNA, snRNA.

### 3. Gene Regulation
*   **Prokaryotic Operons**:
    *   **Lac Operon** (Inducible):
        *   **Repression**: No lactose -> Repressor binds Operator -> No transcription.
        *   **Induction**: Lactose present -> Binds repressor -> Repressor releases -> Transcription.
        *   **Activation**: Low Glucose -> High cAMP -> cAMP-CAP complex binds promoter -> Boosts transcription.
    *   **Trp Operon** (Repressible):
        *   High Tryptophan -> Trp binds Repressor -> Repressor binds Operator -> Block transcription.
*   **Eukaryotic Regulation**:
    *   **Transcriptional**: Enhancers/Silencers (distal elements), Mediators, Activators/Repressors.
    **Post-Transcriptional (Eukaryote)**:
    *   **mRNA Processing (pre-mRNA $\rightarrow$ mature mRNA)**:
        *   **5' Capping**: 7-methylguanosine cap (stability, translation initiation).
        *   **Splicing**: Spliceosome (snRNPs) removes introns, joins exons.
        *   **3' Polyadenylation**: Poly-A tail (stability, nuclear export).
    *   **miRNA/siRNA**:
        *   **miRNA Pathway**:
            *   **pri-miRNA**: Primary transcript (hairpin-structured).
            *   **Nuclear Processing**: **Drosha** (RNase III) cleaves pri-miRNA into **pre-miRNA** (~70nt hairpin).
            *   **Cytoplasmic Processing**: Exportin-5 transports pre-miRNA to cytosol; **Dicer** cleaves it into mature miRNA (~22nt double-stranded).
            *   **RISC**: RNA-induced silencing complex binds miRNA; imperfect match $\rightarrow$ translational repression.
        *   **siRNA**: Perfect match $\rightarrow$ mRNA degradation (Argonaute/RISC).
