# Chapter 8: Classical Mendelian Genetics

## 1. Traits Controlled by a Single Gene
- **Complete Dominance**: The dominant allele completely masks the recessive one.
    - **Ratio (F2)**: 3:1 (e.g., Yellow x Green -> F1: Yellow -> F2: 3 Yellow : 1 Green).
- **Incomplete Dominance (Intermediate Inheritance)**: The heterozygote has an intermediate phenotype.
    - **Ratio (F2)**: 1:2:1 (e.g., Red x White -> F1: Pink -> F2: 1 Red : 2 Pink : 1 White).
- **Codominance**: Both alleles are expressed equally.
    - **Example**: AB blood type (both A and B antigens are present).
- **Pleiotropy**: A single gene influences multiple, seemingly unrelated phenotypic traits.

---

## 2. Polygenic Inheritance (Non-allelic Gene Interaction)
- **Complementary Interaction (9:7 Ratio)**: Non-allelic genes must both be present to produce a specific phenotype.
    - **F2 (9:7)**: `A_B_` (Red); `A_bb`, `aaB_`, `aabb` (White).
- **Collaboration / New Phenotypes (9:3:3:1 Ratio)**:
    - **F2 (9:3:3:1)**: `A_B_` (Walnut); `A_bb` (Rose); `aaB_` (Pea); `aabb` (Single).
- **Additive Interaction / Duplicate Interaction with Cumulative Effect (9:6:1 Ratio)**:
    - **F2 (9:6:1)**: `A_B_` (Flattened); `A_bb`, `aaB_` (Round); `aabb` (Elongated).

---

## 3. Epistatic Gene Interaction
One gene (epistatic) masks or suppresses the expression of another gene (hypostatic).
1. **Dominant Suppression (13:3 Ratio)**:
    - **F2 (13:3)**: `_ _I_` (White); `C_ii` (Black).
2. **Dominant Epistasis (12:3:1 Ratio)**:
    - **F2 (12:3:1)**: `R_ _ _` (Red); `rrY_` (Yellow); `rryy` (White).
3. **Recessive Epistasis (9:3:4 Ratio)**:
    - **F2 (9:3:4)**: `A_B_` (Brownish-gray); `A_bb` (Black); `aaB_`, `aabb` (White).

- **Additive Effects**: The phenotype depends on the cumulative number of dominant alleles present, regardless of their locus.
