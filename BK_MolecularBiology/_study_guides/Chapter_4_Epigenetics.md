# Chapter 4: Epigenetics

### 1. DNA Methylation (Eukaryote)
*   **Mechanism**: Addition of methyl group to Cytosine/Adenine.
*   **Context**: Occurs at CpG regions (G following C). 90-98% of CpG sites are methylated in mammals.
*   **Enzymes (DNMTs)**:
    *   **DNMT1**: Maintains methylation (mother to daughter cells).
    *   **DNMT3a/3b**: De novo methylation.
    *   **DNMT3L**: Supports DNMT3a/3b, inhibits transposons.

### 2. Histone Modification
*   **Nucleosome**: 147 bp DNA wrapped 1.67 times around histone octamer (2x H2A, H2B, H3, H4).
*   **Effect**: Modifications on H3/H4 tails affect DNA-histone interaction and gene expression (open vs. closed chromatin).

### 3. Chromatin Structure
*   **Heterochromatin**: Condensed, transcriptionally repressed.
*   **Euchromatin**: Open/relaxed, transcriptionally active.
*   **3D Folding**: Brings regulatory factors closer to target genes.

### 4. Non-coding RNAs (ncRNAs)
*   **Short**: miRNA, siRNA, piRNA.
*   **Long**: lncRNA (e.g., acts as guide, scaffold/architect, or enhancer).

### 5. Prokaryotic Epigenetics
*   **R-M System**: Restriction-Modification system (REase + MTase) to protect against foreign DNA (phage).
*   **Dam/Dcm Methylation**:
    *   **Dam Methylase**: Methylates Adenine (GATC sequence). Regulates replication (Ori), mismatch repair (MutS/L), and gene expression.
    *   **Orphan MTases**: Operate independently (e.g., CcrM cell cycle regulator).
