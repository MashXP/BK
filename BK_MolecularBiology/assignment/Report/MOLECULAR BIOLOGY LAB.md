**MOLECULAR BIOLOGY & GENETICS EXPERIMENTS\
**\
The course helps students assess the impact of UV on bacterial DNA, DNA
extraction, PCR, and electrophoresis, thereby gaining a deeper
understanding of theoretical lectures on molecular biology.\
**Week 1:** Bacterial Cultivation\
**Week 2:** UV Treatment, DNA Extraction\
**Week 3:** PCR, Electrophoresis\
**Week 4:** Discussion & Exam

**WEEK 1: BACTERIAL CULTIVATION**\
Practical work involves culturing bacteria from a Petri dish into a
liquid MRS medium. Incubate at 37°C for 12 hours.

**WEEK 2: UV TREATMENT AND DNA EXTRACTION**\
Expose the bacteria to UV for 30 minutes. After UV exposure, incubate
the bacteria at room temperature for 15, 30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes.

-   Groups place the bacteria cultured in MRS from Week 1 onto a Petri
    dish, leaving the lid open, and expose them to the UV source for 30
    minutes.

-   Collect the UV-treated bacterial solution (make sure to create a
    bacterial suspension before adding it to the Eppendorf tube).

-   Centrifuge at 6000g for 15 minutes to collect biomass (each group
    will incubate for a different time after UV exposure, with 2 groups
    doing a control without UV treatment).

-   Perform DNA extraction using Trizol:

    -   Add 1mL of Trizol to the biomass in the Eppendorf tube and wait
        for 5 minutes.

    -   Add 200uL of chloroform, close the lid, and shake vigorously for
        15 seconds.

    -   Incubate for 3 minutes at room temperature.

    -   Centrifuge at 12000g for 15 minutes at 4°C.

    -   The centrifuge tube will separate into 3 layers; collect the
        bottom layer, avoiding mixing with the two upper phases.

    -   Add 500uL of Back Extraction Buffer (BEB) and invert the tube
        continuously for 10 minutes at room temperature.

    -   Centrifuge at 12000g for 30 minutes at room temperature.

    -   Transfer the top phase into a new tube, add 400uL of cold
        isopropanol, invert, and incubate at room temperature for 5
        minutes.

    -   Centrifuge at 12000g for 15 minutes at 4°C.

    -   Discard the supernatant (usually, no DNA precipitate will be
        visible---don't worry!).

    -   Add 500uL of 70% ethanol and invert the tube.

    -   Centrifuge at 12000g for 15 minutes at 4°C.

    -   Remove as much of the supernatant as possible.

    -   Let the tube dry for about an hour (the drying time depends on
        whether the sample dries quickly or slowly---don't think about
        drying it with heat!).

    -   Add 50uL of TE buffer and store at -20°C.

**ELECTROPHORESIS**\
**Preparing the Agarose Gel:**

-   Weigh 0.5 g of agarose and add 50 mL of 1X TAE, gently shake to
    evenly disperse the agarose, then heat in the microwave for 2
    minutes.

-   Let it cool to 60°C, then carefully pour the solution into the
    electrophoresis tray. After about 45 minutes, the gel will solidify
    and turn opaque white.

**Performing Electrophoresis:**

-   Load the sample (10 µL + 2 µL of loading gelred).

-   Use a 100 bp ladder.

-   Set the voltage to 110 mV for 30-45 minutes.

-   Observe the electrophoresis results under a UV.
